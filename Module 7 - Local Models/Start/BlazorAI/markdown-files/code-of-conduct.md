# Code of Conduct

---

## 1. Respect and Professionalism

- All employees must maintain respectful behavior.
- Harassment and discrimination are not tolerated.

---

## 2. Use of Company Resources

- Company resources must be used solely for work-related purposes.
- Installing unauthorized software is prohibited.

---

## 3. Conflicts of Interest

- Employees must disclose any conflicts of interest.
- Working with direct competitors without authorization is not allowed.

---

## 4. Confidentiality

- Company information is confidential.
- It must not be shared with third parties without authorization.

---

## 5. Consequences

- Non-compliance may result in disciplinary actions or termination.
