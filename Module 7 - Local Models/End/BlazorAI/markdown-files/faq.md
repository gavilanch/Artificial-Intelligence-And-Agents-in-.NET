# Internal Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

---

## Vacation

**How many vacation days do I get after completing one year?**  
You are entitled to 15 working days.

**How far in advance should I request them?**  
At least 15 calendar days in advance.

---

## Remote Work

**How many days can I work remotely?**  
Up to 3 days per week.

---

## Equipment

**How do I request a new laptop?**  
You must create a ticket in the technical support system.

---

## Technical Support

**What are the technical support hours?**  
Monday through Friday, from 8:00 AM to 6:00 PM.

---

## Security

**Can I share my password?**  
No, it is strictly prohibited.
