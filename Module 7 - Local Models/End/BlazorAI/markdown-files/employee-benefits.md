# Employee Benefits

---

## 1. Health Insurance

- All employees receive health insurance coverage starting from their first day.
- The plan includes both national and international coverage.

---

## 2. Annual Bonus

- An annual performance-based bonus is granted.
- The bonus may range between **1 and 3 monthly salaries**.

---

## 3. Additional Paid Days Off

- Employees receive **2 additional paid days off** per year for personal well-being.
- These days are non-cumulative.

---

## 4. Training

- The company covers up to **$1,000 USD per year** for courses or certifications.
- They must be related to the employee’s role.

---

## 5. Equipment

- A laptop, monitor, and accessories are provided as needed.
- Equipment is renewed every **3 years**.
