# Internal Policies Manual

---

## 1. Vacation Policy

### 1.1 Vacation Days Based on Seniority
- Upon completing **1 year of service**, the employee is entitled to **15 working days of paid vacation**.
- Starting from the second year, **1 additional day is added for each year worked**, up to a maximum of 20 days.

### 1.2 Vacation Requests
- Vacation requests must be submitted at least **15 calendar days in advance**.
- Requests must be made through the internal human resources system.
- The direct manager must approve the request before confirmation.

---

## 2. Remote Work

### 2.1 General Policy
- Employees may work remotely for up to **3 days per week**.
- Remote workdays must be coordinated in advance with the immediate supervisor.

### 2.2 Exceptions
- Special cases may be approved by Human Resources.
- Critical teams may have additional restrictions based on operational needs.

---

## 3. Equipment and Technology Resources

### 3.1 Requesting a New Laptop
- To request a new laptop, the employee must:
  1. Create a ticket in the technical support system.
  2. Justify the need (damage, obsolescence, role change, etc.).
- The IT team will evaluate the request within **3 to 5 business days**.

### 3.2 Equipment Maintenance
- Employees are responsible for the proper use of assigned equipment.
- Any incident must be reported immediately.

---

## 4. Technical Support

### 4.1 Support Hours
- Technical support is available:
  - **Monday through Friday**
  - **8:00 AM to 6:00 PM**
- Outside these hours, only critical incidents will be addressed.

### 4.2 Support Channels
- Ticketing system (preferred)
- Email
- Internal chat (for urgent incidents)

---

## 5. Information Security

### 5.1 Use of Credentials
- Sharing passwords with anyone, including coworkers, is **strictly prohibited**.
- Each employee is responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of their credentials.

### 5.2 Best Practices
- Change passwords periodically.
- Do not reuse passwords on external services.
- Report suspicious access immediately.

---

## 6. Compliance

- Failure to comply with these policies may result in disciplinary action.
- For questions or clarifications, contact the Human Resources or IT department as appropriate.
