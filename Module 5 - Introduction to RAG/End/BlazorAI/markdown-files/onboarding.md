# Onboarding Process

---

## 1. First Day of Work

- The employee must report to the office at **9:00 AM** or connect remotely, as applicable.
- A welcome session will be conducted with Human Resources.
- Access credentials for internal systems will be assigned.

---

## 2. Equipment Delivery

- The IT team will provide a corporate laptop on the first day.
- The employee must sign an acknowledgment of receipt document.
- In the case of remote work, the equipment will be shipped within 3 business days.

---

## 3. Initial Setup

- The employee must:
  - Change the initial password.
  - Configure multi-factor authentication (MFA).
  - Install the required tools (IDE, VPN, antivirus).

---

## 4. Initial Training

- During the first week, mandatory courses will be assigned:
  - Information security
  - Organizational culture
  - Internal tools

---

## 5. Probation Period

- The probation period lasts **3 months**.
- Monthly performance evaluations will be conducted.
